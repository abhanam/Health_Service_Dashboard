
# Gaza Health Service Utilization Dashboard

Interactive dashboard analyzing 147,430 health service encounters at
CARE International’s Deir Al-Balah Primary Health Care Center in Gaza
during 2025.

## Project Overview

This dashboard visualizes healthcare access patterns across four service
categories:

- Primary Health Activity (PH Monthly)
- Health Promotion
- Psychosocial Support
- Nutrition Services

Data is stratified by age group and sex to identify service gaps and
inform resource allocation during active conflict conditions.

## Key Findings

- Health promotion activities accounted for 43% of total service
  utilization
- The 19-49 age group showed highest engagement (48% PH Monthly, 79%
  Health Promotion)
- Women comprised 60% of PH Monthly and 68% of Health Promotion
  participants
- Psychosocial services showed disruption in Q4 2025

## Dashboard Features

- Interactive plotly visualizations with tooltips
- Age and sex stratified analysis
- Monthly trends across service categories
- Mobile responsive design

## Impact

This dashboard enables CARE International and health officials to
identify service gaps and optimize resource allocation in
conflict-affected Gaza. Understanding utilization patterns helps ensure
vulnerable populations receive essential primary healthcare services.

## Data Source

CARE International - Deir Al-Balah PHCC, Gaza (Confidential)
